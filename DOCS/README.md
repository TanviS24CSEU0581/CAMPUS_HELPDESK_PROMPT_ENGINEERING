# Campus Helpdesk Assistant - Policy Sources

This folder contains the policy knowledge base for the Campus Helpdesk Assistant project.

The chatbot uses these sources to answer student queries related to fees, examinations, attendance and hostel policies.

## 1. Fee Structure

- **Source:** Bennett University - Fee Structure
- **Link:** https://www.bennett.edu.in/admission/fee-structure/
- **Collection Date:** 30 October 2026
- **Type:** Official University Website
- **Coverage:** B.Tech CSE tuition fees, hostel fees and other applicable charges.

## 2. Fee Refund Policy

- **Source:** Bennett University Fee Refund Policy - Academic Year 2026-27
- **Document:** Fee-Refund-Policy-26-27.pdf
- **Collection Date:** 30 October 2026
- **Type:** Official University Policy PDF
- **Coverage:** Admission cancellation, tuition fee refund, security deposit refund and hostel charges refund.

## 3. Hostel Policy Notifications

- **Source:** Bennett University - Hostel Policy Decisions and Amendments from AY 2026-27 onwards
- **Notification Dates:** 29 June 2026 and 3 July 2026
- **Collection Date:** 30 October 2026
- **Type:** Official University Notifications
- **Coverage:** Hostel refund policy and advance payment of annual hostel fees.

## 4. Standing Orders for Hostel

- **Source:** Bennett University - Standing Orders for Hostel
- **Document Date:** 6 December 2016
- **Collection Date:** 30 October 2026
- **Type:** Official University Hostel Rules Document
- **Coverage:** Hostel discipline, room maintenance, visitors, mess rules, outpass procedures and student responsibilities.
- **Note:** This document is dated 2016. Current applicability of these rules should be verified with the University.

## 5. Examination Manual

- **Source:** Bennett University - Examination Manual
- **Link:** https://www.bennett.edu.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Examination-Manual-2024-25-.pdf
- **Collection Date:** 30 October 2026
- **Type:** Official University Examination Manual
- **Coverage:** Attendance requirements, examination eligibility, supplementary examinations and re-evaluation.

## Important Notes

- Policy sections are assigned unique IDs in the policy knowledge base.
- Policy wording is retained from the respective university sources.
- The Standing Orders for Hostel document is from 2016 and may require verification.
- Hostel refund notifications and the existing refund policy contain conditions that may need clarification regarding their applicability.
- The chatbot should not invent answers when information is unavailable in the collected documents.
